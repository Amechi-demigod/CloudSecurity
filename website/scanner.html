Our scanner

Seeing Sentrigis in your logs?

If your logs show the user agent Sentrigis-AttackSurface/1.0 or connections from 100.56.83.158, this page explains what it is and how to reach us.

What it is

Sentrigis is a Nigerian cybersecurity company. Our attack surface scanner checks internet-facing systems for security weaknesses, on behalf of a Sentrigis customer who has asked us to check their own domains and addresses. It is a careful, non-intrusive check, the same kind of outside view an attacker would get.

We only scan with proof of ownership

Before anything is scanned, the customer must prove they control it. A domain needs a DNS record or a verification file that only its owner can add, and that proof is checked again at the start of every scan. An IP address is scanned only if a verified domain points to it, if it belongs to the customer's own connected cloud account, or if the customer confirmed they are authorised to test it and a Sentrigis administrator approved the request. Every approval is recorded.

What it does, and what it never does

What it does

Connects to common service ports, reads the greeting a service sends and the details of its TLS certificate, checks web server headers, and sends harmless requests to see whether well-known sensitive files are exposed. For those files it reads at most the first 256 bytes, and never stores the contents.

What it never does

No exploits, no injection or attack payloads, no attempts to break in, and no downloading of data. The only login check is a short list of manufacturers' default passwords on web login pages, and only when the system's owner has switched that check on for their own system.

Gentle by design

At most 20 connection attempts per second and 10 at a time to any one host. A scan stops after one hour at the latest, and most customers scan once a week.

How to recognise it

Source address

100.56.83.158

User agent

Sentrigis-AttackSurface/1.0 (+https://sentrigis.com/scanner)

Ownership record

A DNS TXT record starting sentrigis-verification= on the domain or on _sentrigis. plus the domain, or the file /.well-known/sentrigis-verification.txt.

Think we scanned you by mistake?

If you believe we scanned a system that belongs to you without your permission, contact us with the IP address or domain and the time you saw the traffic. We will investigate, tell you what we find, and block that address from future scans.
