Assets & identity

Every device and account, from purchase to disposal.

Sentrigis keeps an IT asset register that the agent keeps up to date, discovers what your directories and network know about, and points out the accounts and machines that need attention.

Request a demo See discovery sources

IT asset register

Devices with the agent are added and kept current automatically, with the signed-in user as owner.

Every detail in one record

Asset tag, serial number, model, assignee and department, location, purchase date, supplier and cost, warranty end and refresh date, plus your own fields.

Lifecycle

In stock, assigned, in repair, awaiting retrieval, retrieved, lost, stolen and disposed, with a full history of every change. Warranty expiry is flagged ahead of time.

Offboarding

A record for each leaver with a retrieval due date, so overdue devices stand out.

Disposal

Record how each device left (recycled, destroyed, resold, donated or returned) and keep the wipe or destruction certificate on file.

Import and export

Bring an existing register in from CSV and export it at any time.

Discovery

Sentrigis compares what your directories and network know about with the devices it manages, so unmanaged machines stand out.

Active Directory

Read-only LDAP or LDAPS, through a small Windows collector on your network.

Entra ID and Intune

Users, devices and roles through Microsoft Graph, read-only.

Google Workspace

Users, admin roles, ChromeOS and mobile devices, read-only.

Network scans

The collector finds devices on the subnets you choose, checks common ports and reads SNMP v2c or v3 read-only. Run them on demand, daily or weekly, and review the results before acting on them.

Account and device hygiene

Computers

Stale or disabled computers that are still active, devices without the agent, devices missing from Intune and machines that are not domain-joined.

Users

Disabled or long-unused accounts that are still signing in (needs Entra ID P1 or P2 sign-in data).

Privileged accounts

Admin accounts that are disabled or stale, or whose main device has no agent.

Network devices

Default SNMP communities, Telnet or plain-HTTP management, firmware past end of support, and new unmanaged devices on switch ports.
